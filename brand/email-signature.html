Email signature

Table-based with inline styles only, built for Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail. To install, open this page in a browser, select everything inside one white panel, copy, and paste it into the signature field of your mail client. Replace “Nume Prenume” and “Funcție” with your own details.

  • The logo is loaded from https://frigofood.vercel.app/brand/logo/ffd-logo-horizontal.png (PNG, 2× resolution). Gmail and Outlook do not display SVG images, so the PNG is the default. The vector master is https://frigofood.vercel.app/brand/logo/ffd-logo-horizontal.svg; swap it in only for clients that support SVG (Apple Mail).
  • Fonts fall back to Arial in clients that block web fonts. That is expected.
