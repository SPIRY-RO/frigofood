01 · Logo

The FFD mark

Two forward-leaning Fs and an open D, cut at one shared 18.4° slant: motion, lanes, a cold chain that keeps moving. Navy carries the name, blue carries the energy, silver closes the shape like a reefer door. The wordmark is drawn, not typed, so it renders the same everywhere. Always use the supplied files.

Keep a free zone of x on every side, where x is the cap height of “FRIGO FOOD” (about a quarter of the mark’s height). No text, edges or other logos inside it.

04 · Voice and tone

Say less, prove more

We talk like a good dispatcher: short sentences, exact facts, calm under pressure. We write in Romanian and English with the same tone.

Precise

Lanes, temperatures, licence numbers. Facts we can stand behind, never guesses.

Calm

Cold chain is serious work. No exclamation marks, no hype, no urgency theatre.

Direct

Lead with what the client needs: route, load type, how to reach us.

We say

Transport frigorific internațional, FTL și grupaj, pentru carne, pește, fructe, legume și lactate.International refrigerated transport, FTL and groupage, for meat, fish, fruit, vegetables and dairy.

Rute principale între România și Polonia, Olanda, Belgia, Germania, Spania, Franța, Italia, Ungaria, Austria, Cehia, Slovacia, Danemarca, Suedia, Bulgaria și Marea Britanie.Core routes between Romania and Poland, the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Spain, France, Italy, Hungary, Austria, Czechia, Slovakia, Denmark, Sweden, Bulgaria and the UK.

Sunați-ne la +40 757 732 732, +40 741 153 154 sau +40 752 018 060.Call us on +40 757 732 732, +40 741 153 154 or +40 752 018 060.

„Cei mai buni transportatori din Europa!”Superlatives and claims we cannot prove.
